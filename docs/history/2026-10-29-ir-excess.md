# IR / lowering excess before regalloc on regex-syntax (2026-10-29)

Burst follow-up to `2026-10-29-code-size.md` (branch `burst/code-size`).
regex-syntax 0.8.11, `-O -Ccodegen-units=1 --emit=obj`, aarch64, this tree's
debug dylib, defaults: `__text` 334,572 B (LLVM 187,120). All byte numbers below
are `size -m` `__text` of that object; the compile is deterministic. Regalloc
perturbation makes any single change good for about +-1 KB of noise.

Scripts (scratchpad `ie/`): `pat.py` (per-function pattern features over
`llvm-objdump`), `same.py` (call-structure-matched pairs), `callee.py` (which
callees LLVM leaves out of line that we inline), `ngram.py` (mnemonic n-gram
excess), `runs.py`/`ss.py` (copy runs), `mix.py` (mnemonic mix per flag),
`mk2.sh`/`sweep.sh`/`par.sh` (build the object with env vars; `par.sh` sweeps
every `PLIRON_*` knob with `=0`, 378 builds, 6 s each).

## 1. What the extra instructions are not

- **Not per-instruction quality.** Pairing the 46 shared functions of >= 100
  instructions whose `bl` counts match LLVM's (+-30%), built with `PLIRON_INLINE=0`:
  pliron 12,422 instructions vs LLVM 17,190. Over the 155 matched functions of
  >= 10 instructions, pliron 8,376 vs LLVM 9,630. The only classes that stay
  excess in matched pairs are stack traffic (+1.1k) and `mov` (+0.4k), both regalloc.
- **Not load CSE.** Redundant `ldr` of the same non-stack address inside a
  straight line: 2 in the whole object. (The 489 "redundant" loads a naive count
  shows are `[sp]` reloads, regalloc.)
- **Not over-inlining in general.** Callees LLVM calls more often than we do
  (we inlined where it did not): 11.1k instructions at most. The opposite
  (LLVM inlined, we call): 51.8k. `PLIRON_INLINE=0` saves 12.7 KB, not 100.
- **Not GOT-vs-direct data addressing.** 1,439 `adrp; ldr [x]` GOT loads
  (`___rcg_alloc.N` constants, memcpy/memmove) where LLVM has `adrp; add`:
  same instruction count and size.
- **Not `cmp` + `b.cond` fusion.** `cbz`+`cbnz` is 1,233 vs LLVM 1,389; the `cmp x,#0; b.eq`
  pattern that should be `cbz` is absent in both. LLVM has more `cbz`/`subs`/`cmn`/`ccmp`
  mnemonics, we have more `cmp`+`b.lo`; total conditional branches +2k, compares +2k.
  That excess follows inlining/unrolling duplicates (below), not a missing fusion.

## 2. Ranked attribution (whole object, bytes by kill switch)

The `=0` sweep is the cleanest per-pass attribution: bytes the pass adds. Passes
that exist for speed and have an LLVM analogue are listed with it. "Insns" is the
change of the total instruction count when the switch is off (`mix.py`).

| rank | cause | `__text` B | insns | producing pass | LLVM analogue |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | bounded partial unrolling / peeling of latch-tested loops | -19,488 (`PUNROLL=0`), -8,832 (`PEEL=0`) | -4,891 / -2,207 | `pliron-clif-opt/src/punroll.rs` | LLVM unrolls small loops too, once and not x8 with a tail loop and guard |
| 2 | jump threading (duplicates blocks) | -15,232 | -3,809 | `jumpthread.rs` | JumpThreading, bounded by a dup-cost model |
| 3 | inlining volume (incl. `INLINE_EH` -7,616, `INLINE_EH_INVOKE` -6,848) | -12,688 | -6,515 (+2.4k `mov`) | `pliron-ir-opt/src/inline.rs` | |
| 4 | runtime-size memcpy/memmove fast path (two overlapping 16-byte moves + libc fallback) | -12,352 | -3,094 | `lower.rs::small_copy_or_call` (`MEMFAST`) | LLVM always calls libc |
| 5 | loop rotation header copies | -10,176 | -2,560 | `looprot.rs` | LoopRotate |
| 6 | aggregates promoted to scalar slices and moved slice by slice (`NOCAP` -7,264, `SLOTPROM` -6,752, `SROA_PIN` -3,072) | -6.7 to -7.3 KB each, overlapping | -1.7k..-1.8k, `[sp]` ld+st -1.2k | `sroa.rs`, `slotprom.rs` | LLVM SROA keeps non-promotable memcpys |
| 7 | by-value copies of > 16-slice aggregates as 20 interleaved `ldr/str` pairs | -6,944 (`SROA_MAXSLICES=18`) | -1,741 | `sroa.rs` | memcpy -> `ldp q; stp q` |

| 9 | interleaved copy runs that cannot pair into `ldp/stp` | -352 (`COPYPAIR=1`) | not counted | egraph elaboration + regalloc temp reuse | |
| 10 | `mov #-k; add` for a negative address offset | 353 sites, net **+896** when fixed | | `amode_add` in `inst.isle` | dead end, see below |

Rows 1-6 are speed-for-size trades that already have a kill switch; they explain
about 100 KB of the 147 KB gap and are not "excess" in the sense of redundant work.
Rows 7-10 are what this burst found and flagged.

### Instruction mix of the shared functions (whole object, 329 shared symbols)

LLVM 43,350 vs pliron 66,991 (+23,641): `mov` +6.2k (reg-reg +4.5k), `[sp]` ld +4.6k /
st +2.9k, heap `ldr` +1.7k, `cmp` +1.5k, `b` +1.5k, `and` +0.75k, `uxtb` +0.54k, `cbnz` +0.5k,
`adrp` +0.4k, `nop` +0.27k (loop alignment), `udf` +0.27k, `blr` +0.17k.
Fewer than LLVM: `cbz` -631, `lsl` -242, `subs` -280, `cmn` -284, `ccmp` -95, `csinc` -80, `tbz` -173.

### Copy runs (n-gram and run scan)

`ld xN,[a]; st xN,[b]` runs of >= 3 pairs: 157 runs, 2,002 instructions, LLVM 87 runs,
706 instructions. By source/destination: stack to stack 1,058 (87 runs), stack to heap 414,
heap to stack 240, heap to heap 140. The stack to stack runs are mostly regalloc edge moves of
20 spilled slices of one promoted `Ast` (160 B), not IR copies: the same function has no `ldr/str`
pair in its pre-compile CLIF. The heap runs are real: `ClassSetUnion::push` copies the 160 B
`Ast` as 20 scalar `ldr/str` pairs (SROA `Kind::Escape` fresh copy for the drop-on-panic path
plus the egraph elaborator re-placing each readonly load next to its store) where LLVM emits
five `ldp q; stp q` pairs.

## 3. Flags (default off)

| flag | `__text` B | delta | notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| (baseline) | 334,572 | | |
| `PLIRON_SROA_MAXSLICES=18` | 327,628 | **-6,944** | refuse to scalarize an alloca with more than N typed slices (default 64). Not monotonic: 8 +3,360, 12 +2,784, 14 +3,680, 16 -5,824, 18 -6,944, 20 -3,744, 24 -3,456, 32 -1,568. The win is the 20-slice `Ast`/`Hir` family; a threshold of 16..18 is regex-syntax specific. Needs bench |
| `PLIRON_TBNZ_IREDUCE=1` | 334,316 | -256 | -172 `uxtb`; fewer insns than bytes show because regalloc moves around it |
| `PLIRON_COPYPAIR=1` | 334,220 | -352 | loads-then-stores groups of `i64x2` items; only fires where aliasing is provable |
| all three | 327,020 | -7,552 | |
| all three + `PLIRON_PUNROLL=0` (reference) | 307,436 | -27,136 | the unroll cost |

Dead ends measured:

- `amode_add` negative offsets as `sub #k` (a vendor ISLE rule): the 353 `mov #-k; add` pairs vanish
  (-378 `mov`, -382 `add`) but 942 more `sub` appear and the object grows +896 B net (+260
  instructions): the `sub` cannot be shared like the old materialized constant, and quicksort
  bodies re-allocate worse. Reverted, not in the branch.
- `PLIRON_SROA_MAXSYNC` (limit pin/sync slices around calls): at most -992 B, the same as the
  existing `PLIRON_SROA_SYNC=0`. Reverted.

- `pliron-clif-opt/tests/copypair.rs`: slot-to-slot regroup (shape plus interpreter equivalence),
  overlapping same-slot copy untouched, two unrelated pointers untouched, `readonly` loads regroup
  without `can_move`. The positive tests fail without the pass; the two negative ones fail if the
  legality check is removed.
- `pliron-clif-opt/tests/tbnz_ireduce.rs` (aarch64 asm): `tbnz`/`tbz` with no `uxtb`/`uxth`; two
  of three fail with the knob off (checked by flipping it).
- `PLIRON_SROA_MAXSLICES` only refuses a split, so the fallback is the existing "alloca stays
  in memory" path; no new transform to test.

## 6. Next ideas by bytes

1. Size-aware `PUNROLL` (19.5 KB) / `JUMPTHREAD` (15.2 KB) / `LOOPROT` (10.2 KB) / `PEEL` (8.8 KB): each has a speed
   justification, none has a cold/size gate. Gating on "loop body is cold" (leads to a panic call) or on the function
   being huge would take part of the 54 KB; needs bench per kernel.
2. `MEMFAST` site compaction (12.4 KB over about 180 sites, 68 B each): the fast path costs `sub`, `cmp`, two
   `ldr q`, two `str q`, address recomputation and keeps the slow-path call's argument registers live. Emit it
   only when the size is not provably > 32, or only outside blocks that lead to a panic.
3. Aggregate moves as a memcpy (`ldp q; stp q`): SROA's `Kind::Escape` copy and the generic per-slice
   expansion of copies > 64 B. LLVM keeps these as memcpy. Beyond `MAXSLICES`, an explicit "do not split an
   alloca whose only accesses are whole-object copies" rule would be principled; the 20-slice threshold is a proxy.
4. `select c, 1, 0` / `cset` results returned as `uext i8` still get a `uxtb` (the `subs wzr; csinc; uxtb; ret`
   shape): extend `narrow_uext_clean` to `select` of {0,1} constants and `icmp`.
5. `ireduce.i8` of a call result feeding `cbnz`/`cmp` (`uxtb; cbnz` x49, `uxtb; cmp` x122): the ABI already returns
   `uext`/`zeroext` for bool; trust the callee's extension on Apple arm64 (LLVM does not extend after a `zeroext` call).
6. Loop-alignment `nop`s: `PLIRON_ALIGN=0` -2,432 B (already measured in the previous log).
7. `adrp; ldr` GOT for local `___rcg_alloc.N` data: size-neutral, but a direct `adrp; add` is one fewer
   dependent load per use and removes GOT slots (startup).
