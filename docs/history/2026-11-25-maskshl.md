# `PLIRON_VEC_MASKSHL` default-on; `fold_mask_shifts` learns `imul`

Continuation of the rust-html-rewriter instruction-excess dig (the
`num/mod.rs:715` line: `is_ascii_uppercase() as u8 * 32`).

## What changed

`loopvec::fold_mask_shifts` (`pliron-clif-opt/src/loopvec.rs`):

* `vconst_splat` generalized to `splat_const` — also matches
  `splat(iconst k)`, not just an all-equal `vconst`.
* New `imul` arms next to the existing `ishl` one:
  `imul(band(m, splat 1), C)` -> `band(m, splat C)` and
  `imul(m, C)` -> `band(m, splat -C)` for `m` an `icmp`/`fcmp` lane
  mask (`-1 * C` wraps to `0 - C`; the negation truncates to the lane
  width when the vconst is written).
* Gate moved to `DEFAULT_ON` in `pliron-cfg` (`=0` off), replacing the
  `=1` opt-in.

Test `mask_shift_and_mul_fold_to_band`. Gate: green, 134 jobs.

## Measured

aarch64-linux-musl callgrind exact totals, `irdiff.py --backend
target/debug` (same dylib, `PLIRON_VEC_MASKSHL=0` as the other side):

* rust-html-rewriter: on 92,928,201 vs off 92,928,190 — +11, noise.
  `num/mod.rs:715` unchanged at +725,749.
* pulldown-cmark: on 46,369,410 vs off 46,369,400 — +10, noise.

The fold fires and produces LLVM's exact vector body (verified in the
binary: `sub; cmhi; and #0x20; orr` per vector, splat hoisted; `and
#1; shl #5` gone) but buys ~zero executed instructions **because the
vector loop does not run**: lol_html's lowercase calls are tag/attr
names, almost all under 64 bytes, so execution takes the scalar tail.
The +726k on `num/mod.rs:715` is the scalar path — ~9 insts/byte in
our remainder loop vs LLVM's ~8 (plus a ~20-inst per-call scaffold
LLVM doesn't pay). `Bytes::as_lowercase_string`'s real cost is that
loopvec vectorized a loop whose trips are short; LLVM kept it scalar.
`PLIRON_VEC_REM` (still opt-in: +0.063% html on 2026-11-20) is the
prior attempt at exactly this.

Kept default-on anyway: strictly fewer static instructions
(-344 B .text on the html benchmark), LLVM-identical shape, no
measured downside on either program.
