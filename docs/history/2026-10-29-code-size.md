Part 1 stopped on disk (below 15 GB free); part 2 (below) resumed after the
cleanup. Part 1 numbers come from the older main-checkout debug dylib (Oct 28 19:13; defaults, no card-B flags), regex-syntax
0.8.11, `-O -Ccodegen-units=1 --emit=obj`, aarch64.

| | LLVM | pliron |
|---|---|---|
| `__text` B | 187,120 | 334,572 |
| `PLIRON_ALIGN=0` | | 332,140 (-2,432) |
| `PLIRON_INLINE_GROWTH=100000 PLIRON_INLINE_CALLER_MAX=100000` | | 334,572 (no change) |

Per-function sizes joined on symbol (v0 names): 173,400 B (LLVM) vs 274,476 B
(pliron) in the shared functions, plus **60,096 B in 60 pliron-only functions**
(LLVM has 13,720 B of its own-only functions). Biggest pliron-only: `visit<print::Writer>` 6.0 KB,
`StrSearcher::next_match` 5.8 KB, `ast::visitor::visit<Translator>` 4.7 KB,
`HirKind::eq` 4.5 KB, `HeapVisitor::visit<NestLimiter>` 4.1 KB,
`Extractor::extract_repetition` 3.5 KB, `Hir::dot` 2.8 KB, `class_chars/bytes` 1.9 KB each.
All are local (`t`) with 1-2 call sites. Inline trace (`PLIRON_TRACE=pliron::inline=debug`):
`extract_repetition` is called twice from `Extractor::extract` (n=2346, t=320,
not "free": `single` needs one call site), `Hir::dot` from two callers (n=4619, t=3200 reject).

Mnemonic deltas (count, pliron - LLVM): mov +8,102; ldr +4,300; ldur +3,224
(`ldur`/`stur` are only an encoding of `ldr`/`str`; sum ldr+ldur = +7,500 vs
LLVM 5,000); cmp +2,573; stur +2,472; b +2,237 (4,477 vs 2,240); str +1,909;
bl +1,029; and +871; stp +742 (LLVM pairs more: 2,650 vs 3,392 stp but 4x the
ldp/stp share per insn); cbnz +643; uxtb +629 (LLVM 0); nop +421;
udf +334; LLVM-only: cbz +605, subs +302, cmn +291, ccmp +116, csinc +87.

Observations from `ClassUnicode::push` (936 B LLVM vs 2,592 B):
- Loop-header alignment nops (`PLIRON_ALIGN`, default on): 641 nops vs 220; 2.4 KB.
  A size-aware variant (align only loops with a small body) is the cheap compromise.
- Address folding misses: `lsl x0,x24,#3; add x3,x2,x24,lsl #3; ldr w4,[x2,x0]; ldr w3,[x3,#4]`
  where one `add` plus two immediate-offset loads suffice.
- `memmove` through the GOT: `adrp; ldr; blr` (3 insns, 201 `blr` in the crate) instead of `bl`.
- Loop-carried "previous value" copies (`mov x22,x5; mov x21,x6` every iteration).
- `b` count is 2x LLVM; 2,237 extra. Of 4,477 `b`, 516 land in a `ret`-ending block of 3-6
  insns (epilogue tails; candidates for tail duplication of a bare `ret` epilogue only when
  it is 2 insns) and 769 in another `b`-ending block of <=6 insns (edge blocks).

## Ranked next ideas (untested; bytes are upper-bound estimates)

1. Free-inline a local callee whose call sites are all in one caller (2 sites):
   up to ~30 KB of the 60 KB pliron-only code is such functions; LLVM's net gain
   is the callee prologue/epilogue plus arg shuffling, est. 3-6 KB.
2. Call-through-GOT for `memmove/memcpy` (`blr` 201): direct `bl` through a stub, 8 B each, ~1.6 KB.
3. Address-mode fold for `shifted-index + imm` load pairs (Cranelift aarch64 isle), est. 2-4 KB.
4. Size-aware loop alignment, ~1.5 KB of the 2.4 KB.
5. cbz/cbnz after `cmp #0` / `subs` fusion: LLVM has 605 more cbz; check `cmp x,#0; b.eq` pairs.
6. mov traffic (+8 K insns, 32 KB, the largest class): attribute with `PLIRON_VREGMAP`,
   `PLIRON_RA2_ALLOC` and `tools/raspill.py` once a build is possible.

Scripts: scratchpad `fsz.py` (per-function size join), `mn.py` (mnemonic delta), `bt.py` (branch-target classes).


## Part 2: regalloc attribution and flags (this tree's debug dylib, same input)

Baseline (this tree, defaults; the card-B flags are already default-on, so
`PLIRON_EDGEMERGE`/`EPIMERGE` default to mode 1): `__text` 334,572 B.

New tooling: `PLIRON_RA2_STATS=1` (vendored Cranelift `machinst/compile.rs`)
prints one `RA2STAT` line per function: regalloc2 edits by kind (reg-reg,
spill store, reload) and position (at the block's last inst, at its entry,
elsewhere). With `PLIRON_PAR=0 PLIRON_PREVCODE=all PLIRON_RA2_EDITS=1` the
dump is enough to attribute every edit crate-wide (scratchpad `cls.py`,
`rl.py`; `tools/raspill.py` is single-function and hardcodes /tmp paths).

### Where the instructions are (81,934 total, LLVM 46,780)

| bucket | insns | bytes | note |
|---|---|---|---|
| regalloc reg-reg moves | 9,023 | 36 KB | LLVM's whole `mov` count is 7,896 (incl. immediates) |
| regalloc reloads | 5,790 | 23 KB | 853 slots, 6.8 reloads/slot; 158 slots reloaded 10+ times |
| regalloc spill stores | 2,607 | 10 KB | |
| everything else | 64,514 | 258 KB | vs LLVM 46,780 total: the larger share of the gap is IR/lowering, not regalloc |

Regalloc edits are 17.4k of the 35.2k extra instructions at most; LLVM has its
own ~8k, so the regalloc excess is roughly 9k insns (26% of the gap). "Mostly
register allocation" overstates it.

Reg-reg moves by position (edge blocks = critical-edge `mov; b`):

| position | rr | ld | st |
|---|---|---|---|
| terminator of a real block (phi/arg moves; 679 sit on a `try_call`/`bl`, i.e. call ABI) | 2,016 | 1,019 | 560 |
| edge block (split critical edge) | 1,890 | 1,064 | 444 |
| mid-block, not at a call | 1,352 | 2,048 | 916 |
| mid-block at a call | 1,275 | 319 | 31 |
| block entry | 1,187 | 1,048 | 319 |
| landing-pad edge block (exception edge with payload moves) | 938 | 251 | 327 |
| landing-pad block entry | 365 | 41 | 10 |

Reloads by the defining op of the reloaded vreg (rl.py, noisy): constants
(`movz/movn/movk`) about 110 only, so rematerialization is not the lever;
args about 780 (13%); loaded-from-memory values about 1,000.

Dead ends measured: `PLIRON_INLINE_ONECALLER` (treat a callee whose sites are all in
one caller as single-site, free to inline): +23,840 B, because two sites
duplicate the body; reverted. `PLIRON_RA2_SQK` sweep of regalloc2's spill-weight
sqrt factor (default 8): k=2 -704, 4 -736, 16 +992, 64 +1,792: not a lever.
`PLIRON_ALIGN=0`: -2,432 B.
