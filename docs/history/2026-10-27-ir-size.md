# IR size after inlining: where the ops are, `PLIRON_INLINE_CLEAN`, `PLIRON_INLINE_FANIN`

Follows docs/history/2026-10-27-memory3.md ("fewer ops after inlining" was
the remaining lever). Both new flags are default off. Measurements with the
release dylib on `harness/ctime.py`'s syn and regex-syntax inputs (`-O`, 16
CGUs), `PLIRON_DEADFREE=1` on throughout so dead bodies don't blur the counts.

## 1. Where the ops are

`PLIRON_IR_HIST=<file>` (new, `pliron-ir-opt/src/irstat.rs`) appends a
per-CGU histogram before inlining, after it, and at the end of iropt: ops by
kind, the biggest functions, pure ops with no uses, pure ops repeating an
earlier op of the same block (constants canonicalized), blocks whose only
predecessor edge is an unconditional `br`, and intrinsic names.

syn, summed over 16 CGUs:

| | pre-inline | post-inline | end of iropt |
|---|---|---|---|
| ops in bodies | 325,650 | 1,219,168 | 1,284,672 |
| blocks | 68,568 | 422,418 | 421,123 |
| floating constant placeholders | 237,462 | 237,462 | 368,037 |
| arena ops (`ir_stats`) | 563,112 | 1,456,630 | 1,652,709 |
| pure ops with no use | 7,703 | 71,318 | 83,432 |
| duplicate pure ops (same block) | 472 | 998 | 1,011 |
| single-`br`-pred blocks | 17,061 | 226,157 | 226,613 |
| blocks holding only a `br` | 2,287 | 149,811 | 151,278 |

Ops by kind at the end of iropt (1.28M in bodies): `br` 303,639 (24%),
`call_intrinsic` 152,730 (12%; `llvm.lifetime.start/end` are 90% of them,
`pliron.eh.exn` most of the rest), `load` 147,830, `icmp` 117,695 (67,581 of
them with no use), `store` 111,448, `getelementptr` 100,003, `alloca` 90,411,
`unreachable` 63,894, `call` 58,500, `cond_br` 48,501, `sub` 17,093 (7,938
unused), `select` 13,624, `trunc` 13,748, `mul` 10,596 (6,226 unused).

Reading it:

- **Inlining is the growth: 3.7x in ops, 6.2x in blocks.** The builder's
  output is 325k ops. Constants are a separate 237k `llvm.undef`
  placeholders (42% of the pre-inline arena), but they are *not* cloned by
  inlining (`clone_blocks_into` maps unmapped operands to themselves), so
  they are a fixed cost, 22% of the final arena. The 130k new ones at the
  end are the passes' own (instcombine, sroa, constload).
- **Every fourth op is a `br`, three ops per block.** rustc's MIR blocks
  are tiny, and each inline site adds `br entry(args)` plus a `br cont(v)`
  per callee return. 54% of the post-inline blocks have exactly one
  predecessor edge and it is an unconditional `br`; 35% hold nothing but a
  `br`. LLVM's SimplifyCFG removes both shapes right after inlining.
- **57% of the `icmp`s have no use.** rustc emits `icmp` + `assume(cond)`
  for layout and non-null facts; `intrinsic.rs` drops the assume and the
  compare stays. 7.7k dead ops before inlining become 71k after: every
  clone carries them.
- **Duplicates are not a lever**: 1k same-block repeats in 1.28M ops. A
  local CSE would do nothing here (the CLIF pipeline does it later anyway).
- **Which callees?** `PLIRON_INLINE_VERBOSE=1` on syn: 24,243 accepted
  sites pasted 1,546,225 ops. The top callees by ops pasted:

  | callee | copies | ops each | ops pasted |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | `drop_glue<Vec<Attribute>>` | 1,308 | 110 | 144,129 |
  | `RawVecInner::deallocate` | 1,018 | 59 | 59,766 |
  | `drop_glue<proc_macro::Ident>` | 708 | 73 | 51,684 |
  | `drop_glue<Box<Expr>>` | 788 | 64 | 50,432 |
  | `drop_glue<Visibility>` | 340 | 81 | 27,534 |
  | `drop_glue<Block>` | 240 | 106 | 25,344 |
  | `drop_glue<TokenStream>` | 418 | 60 | 25,080 |
  | `token::parsing::peek_punct` | 42 | 531 | 22,302 |

  Drop glue of parser locals, pasted at every early return. Each sits well
  under the 320-op base limit (cost = ops + 20 per nested call), so the
  cost model accepts every one of its hundreds of sites. LLVM's threshold
  (225 cost units at 5 per instruction, so ~45 instructions at a plain
  site) keeps `drop_in_place::<Vec<Attribute>>` out of line: its syn object
  has it once per CGU.
- **The biggest functions are the parsers that hold those copies.**
  `parse_rest_of_item` 20,835 ops (166 inlined sites, 13,668 ops pasted),
  `ambig_ty` 11,677, `trailer_helper` 11,285, `closure_arg` 6,772 (23 copies
  each of `drop_glue<Vec<Attribute>>` and `drop_glue<[Attribute]>`). The
  per-CGU `drop_glue<Expr>` copies (4,423 ops each, in 10 CGUs) inline the
  whole variant drop tree.

Machine code (per-function `__text` by symbol, `llvm-nm -n` on both rlibs;
pliron sums its per-CGU copies): syn text 1,999,808 B against LLVM's
1,021,384 (1.96x). On the 2,100 shared symbols 1,684,640 vs 774,492 (2.18x).
`parse_rest_of_item` 31,104 vs 8,972 (3.47x); `drop_glue<BareFnArg>` 19,360
vs 244 (79x); `drop_glue<ExprIf>` 8,800 vs 508; `drop_glue<ParseBuffer>`
8,672 vs 1,080. The IR size and the code size have the same cause.

## 2. The two levers

### `PLIRON_INLINE_CLEAN=1` (`pliron-ir-opt/src/cleanup.rs`)

A linear per-function cleanup, run on every body before inlining and on
each caller right after the inliner finishes with it. The inliner is
bottom-up, so a callee is cleaned before it is cloned into its callers and
the saving compounds through the clone chain.

- DCE: an op with results, none used, and no side effect (arithmetic,
  compares, casts, GEPs, aggregates, non-volatile loads) is erased; operands
  that become dead follow through a worklist.
- Block merge: a block whose only predecessor edge is an unconditional
  `br` is spliced onto that predecessor, its arguments replaced by the
  branch operands (LLVM's SimplifyCFG `MergeBlockIntoPredecessor`).

Skipped: the entry block; landing pads (entered by unwinding, not an
edge); a block after an invoke — `call; br` must end the block, or
block-level dominance credits the continuation to the landing pad (the
nu_command `copy_on_thread` miscompile of 2026-10-22); a clone's entry
block, which is a frozen-region id (`region_parent` key, `frozen_loads`
target). Any other block may join its predecessor and take its region:
the merged block can't become a preheader of a loop holding the clone's
tagged loads (a clone is entered only through its entry, from the
callsite block), so licm's in-region test can't newly pass. Under `-g`
the cleanup is off (block maps in the debug info).

The inliner's scores keep counting the uncleaned body (`callee_info` runs
before the cleanup), so the per-caller cleanup changes no verdict; the
pre-inline cleanup does make callees measure smaller, which admits a few
more sites. Unit test: `pliron-ir-opt/tests/cleanup.rs`.

The invoke rule is why only a third of the single-`br`-pred blocks merge:
syn is unwinding-heavy, and most of those blocks are the continuation after
an invoke.

### `PLIRON_INLINE_FANIN=<m>` (`inline.rs`)

At a plain site, a callee is inlined only while
`callee ops x its remaining call sites <= m x the site's threshold`
(threshold = the base limit after the site's bonuses). `Adj::callers_of`
already keeps the module-wide site count current as sites inline and
clones add new ones. Exempt: sites in loops (the bench kernels' hot paths),
`#[inline(always)]`, and single-site callees (free). Order-independent in
effect: a fat many-site callee is kept out of line everywhere, as one body,
instead of the first callers in post-order getting copies. With m=8 and
the 320 base: a 110-op callee stays out of line past 23 sites per CGU, a
25-op one past 102.

## 3. Op counts

syn (16 CGUs, `PLIRON_DEADFREE=1`), summed:

| | base | CLEAN | FANIN=8 | CLEAN + FANIN=8 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ops after inlining | 1,219,168 | 1,019,875 | 828,439 | 765,386 |
| blocks after inlining | 422,418 | 344,913 | 265,754 | 245,109 |
| ops at end of iropt | 1,284,672 | 1,119,297 | 929,999 | 888,099 |
| arena ops at end | 1,652,709 | 1,480,131 | 1,290,461 | 1,245,745 |
| accepted inline sites | 24,243 | | | |
| `__text` bytes | 1,999,712 | 1,976,552 | 1,638,296 | 1,704,528 |

regex-syntax, CLEAN + FANIN=8: ops after inlining 316,049 → 255,795,
blocks 92,001 → 69,433, arena at end 435,599 → 379,376, `__text` 462,076 →
455,428. Fewer high-fan-in callees there; the cleanup carries it.

CLEAN alone: pre-inline erased 7.7k ops and merged 7.1k blocks; the
inliner's per-caller passes erased 63k more and merged 70k. CLEAN + FANIN
grows text 4% over FANIN alone: cleaned callees measure smaller, so more
pass the threshold.

## What's left

- The other two thirds of the single-`br`-pred blocks sit after invokes;
  the 150k `br`-only blocks (clone return blocks, `br cont(v)`) could be
  forwarded to their target (retarget each predecessor's branch, substitute
  the arguments) without touching the invoke boundary.
- Constants: 237k floating `llvm.undef` placeholders before inlining, one
  per use at build time (`CodegenCx::new_value`), 368k at the end. A
  per-CGU intern of `(type, ConstVal)` would cut most of them; sharing is
  safe for `consts` (never removed or mutated), lowering reads the value's
  entry at each use.
- `lifetime.start/end` are 12% of ops after inlining; slotmerge needs them,
  but an alloca promoted by sroa leaves its markers behind.
- The threshold itself: 320 ops against LLVM's ~45-instruction plain-site
  equivalent. FANIN only trims the many-site tail; `parse_rest_of_item`'s
  3.5x code is the remaining per-site growth.
