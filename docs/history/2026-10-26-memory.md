# Compile memory: where it goes, `PLIRON_DEADFREE`, and two pliron fixes

## 1. The gap

Peak RSS at `-O` (`/usr/bin/time -l`, `harness/ctime.py`'s commands):

| input | LLVM | pliron |
|---|---|---|
| regex-syntax | 280 MiB | 968 MiB |
| syn | 609 MiB | 2,434 MiB |
| regex-wasm | 253 MiB | 964 MiB |

The pliron flag set (slot, inliner, ESCFLOW, REMAT) doesn't change these.

## 2. Where it goes

- `-Ztime-passes`: on syn, `codegen_crate` takes RSS 294 → 2,195 MiB in
  about a second (LLVM: +134 MiB). Later phases don't raise the peak.
- `PLIRON_MEM_MARK=1` (new) adds the process's `phys_footprint` to every
  `PLIRON_TRACE_FILE` mark and every `pt!` pass. The timeline shows each
  CGU adding 80–220 MiB while it is defined and its stage A runs. All 16 are
  resident together: rustc defines them faster than the pool finishes
  them, and stage B starts in fact-publish order.
- Serial (`PLIRON_CGUPAR=0`): 804 MiB, 10.6 s, against 2,449 MiB and 3.7 s.
  So the size of each CGU's IR, times all of them being in flight, is the
  cost. With `-Ccodegen-units=1` it is 1,677 MiB.
- With jemalloc decay off (`_RJEM_MALLOC_CONF=dirty_decay_ms:0,muzzy_decay_ms:0`)
  the peak drops 20% (2,428 → 1,950). The remainder is live memory.
- Per CGU (`ir_stats`, `mem_report`): syn's cgu.00 has 43,733 ops after
  lowering and 193,919 ops / 58,287 blocks after stage A. At publish,
  `Context`'s containers account for 94 MB of its ~115: the op arena
  43 MB, blocks 11, link/kind tables 15, pools 18. The operand and result
  pools hold about one live entry per slot, so orphaned spans are not the
  problem. `Operation` is 160 B (168 in its `RefCell`).
- Op count against LLVM: LLVM's optimized IR for syn is 160k instructions.
  Our 16 CGUs hold 2.7M ops at publish (575k after lowering).
- At publish 43% of the ops in bodies were functions nothing references.
  Most were `dead_unopt` xcgu copies: local copies of other CGUs' functions
  made for inlining and used up by it, then kept and emitted unoptimized,
  because `dead_fns` exempts xcgu copies. The exemption dates from a
  dangling-`U` link bug (DEBUG-LOG, e111bdd). Its counting blindspot has
  since been fixed in `indirect_callee`. With `PLIRON_XCGUFN_KILL=1` the
  historical repro (`cranelift-assembler-x64-meta`'s generator, default
  CGUs, `-O`) links, and its 145,601-line output is byte-identical.
- Facts are small (about 1,000 symbols per CGU), not the retention.

## 3. `PLIRON_DEADFREE=1`

Default off.

- `inline::free_dead` replaces `mark_dead_unopt` at the three `earlydead`
  points. It frees, not marks, every `Local` fn with no remaining direct
  caller and no taken address, xcgu copies included, and iterates.
- In the inliner, a local callee whose last call was just inlined is freed
  at once, as LLVM's inliner deletes it. Its own calls are dropped from
  `Adj`, which can free its callees. Freed arena slots are reused by the
  clones still to come, so the arena doesn't keep growing; freeing after
  the inliner had saved almost nothing, because the arena had already
  peaked. `State::purge_dead` sweeps every op-, block- and value-keyed side
  table, after every 20,000 freed ops and at the end of each run.
- Lowering already panics on an unknown symbol. A reference to a freed
  function now says so ("reference to dead-stripped local fn …"), a
  compile error rather than a link failure.

syn's cgu.00 at publish: 194k → 107k ops, arena 43 → 21 MB. All CGUs:
2.7M → 1.69M ops.

| input | pliron | DEADFREE | DEADFREE + `PLIRON_CGU_INFLIGHT=8` | LLVM |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| kernels | 270 MiB 0.28 s | 240 MiB 0.28 s | 246 MiB 0.29 s | 149 MiB 0.49 s |
| kernels-wasm | 323 0.68 | 259 0.62 | 253 0.64 | 129 0.60 |
| regex-syntax | 984 1.46 | 793 1.52 | 758 1.62 | 295 1.66 |
| regex-wasm | 962 3.03 | 773 2.77 | 780 2.79 | 254 2.97 |
| syn | 2,582 3.97 | 1,991 3.93 | 1,672 4.18 | 574 3.48 |

Output shrinks by about a quarter (syn 9,409 → 7,121 KiB of objects),
since the dead copies are no longer emitted.

## 4. `PLIRON_CGU_INFLIGHT=N`

`submit` blocks rustc's main thread while N CGUs are submitted and
unfinished. It is off under the `XFACTS_ALL` barrier, which needs every CGU
published first. Finishing needs only lower ordinals, so the cap can't
deadlock. It trades time for memory: syn cap 4 1,330 MiB / 4.64 s, cap 2
998 / 6.48 (unlimited 2,490 / 3.71). Output is byte-identical at every cap
(docs/determinism.md R3).

## 5. Two pliron fixes the freeing exposed

- **Stale link fields.** Dense `op_links`/`block_links` entries carry the
  occupant's version, so a stale entry reads as empty. But `update_links`
  changed one field (say `prev`) and stamped the new version, keeping the
  previous occupant's other field (`next`). `BasicBlock::erase` deallocates
  its ops while still linked, so a reused slot inherited a stale `next`,
  and list insertion panicked (`next.get_prev(ctx).unwrap()`). A stale entry
  is now reset first. Test: `pliron-ir-opt/tests/erase_reuse.rs`, which
  fails without the fix. Any pass that erases a whole block could have hit
  this.
- **Side tables.** Passes iterate side-table keys (`nounwind` walks
  `invokes`, `address_taken` asks every `consts` value `is_used`), so bulk
  erasure needs `State::purge_dead`. New vendored APIs: `ColMap`/`ColSet`
  `retain`, `Ptr::is_live` and `Value::is_live`.

## 6. Checks

Gate passes with `DEADFREE` alone and with every flag. `test.sh deadfree`
builds tests/std at 8 CGUs under it: it must match stock rustc's output and
log freed ops. Fuzz 9300–9699 with every flag plus DEADFREE passes. Crates
tier 1 with every flag plus DEADFREE: 20 crates, no pliron-only failures.

Clean stage 2 with every flag plus DEADFREE, peak `phys_footprint` per
crate (`memsample.py`, the summed peak over concurrently compiling
crates):

| | before | DEADFREE |
|---|---|---|
| all crates at once | 20,306 MiB | 13,465 MiB (−34%) |
| `rustc_query_impl` | 14,554 | 9,401 (−35%) |
| `rustc_trait_selection` | 13,884 | 9,506 (−32%) |
| stage-2 compile | 858 s | 800 s |

All stage-2 repros pass. Stack needs are unchanged or better
(expr-stack-overflow 11 MiB).

## Open

- The remaining gap is the IR's size. syn is still 1,991 MiB against LLVM's
  574, because 16 CGUs' post-inlining IR is live at once, each 5–10x
  LLVM's. Next levers: fewer ops after inlining (2.7M against LLVM's 160k
  instructions; constants as separate ops account for part), and smaller
  per-op side data.
- About 15 MB per CGU is retained after the CGU finishes, even with jemalloc
  decay off. Not yet traced.
